Voyage Content Factory is only part-built: nothing new shows in the preview yet, because the page isn't hooked up.

Built so far:
3D coverflow assembly line: all seven stages with counts and stage colours, a larger selected card, spring transitions, arrow and keyboard navigation, and drag onto a stage. On narrow screens it switches to a snap-scrolling strip. Each card shows a preview that fits its stage.
Selected item cockpit: Brief / Script / Scenes / Versions tabs that change content. You can edit the hook, script, item brief, per-platform captions, destinations and schedule. It also has scene thumbnails with "set thumbnail" and "request real footage", version history, and an activity trail. The Muninn panel has Pause item / Resume / Steer / Retry / Send back, and a steering box that pauses the task and shows a simulated replan.
Human approval rules: only You or Cody can approve; Muninn can't. Posting stays locked without a valid approval, and drag or move actions can't skip the gate. Any material edit after approval makes it stale and records who approved, when, which revision, and what changed. Request changes sends the item back to Editing with your note.
Simulated demo progression: factory-wide pause stops all progress and dispatch, and item pause stops that item. Posting tracks each platform separately: queued, scheduled, published with a receipt marked simulated, failed (one deliberate WonPlace X failure you can retry), and held. TideLine's company LinkedIn is a source-only record, and its other five destinations show as not connected and held.
Seeded demo content: campaigns across every stage for both brands, using the new gym and service-business photos. "Training / Retaining" is selected in Editing, and two items are waiting for approval. Edits survive a refresh, and Reset demo restores the starting state.
Analytics demo data: "Total reported views" sums views only; rates stay per platform, missing metrics show as unavailable, and outcomes come from the CRM, with followers never counted as leads. There are also insight cards that can become new briefs.
