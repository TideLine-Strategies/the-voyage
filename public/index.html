Connect your calendar

Show your own meetings next to your Voyage appointments, so you can spot conflicts before you book. Only you see them, and The Voyage never changes your calendar.

Google Calendar
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer, then Settings (gear icon).
  2. Under "Settings for my calendars", pick your calendar, then "Integrate calendar".
  3. Copy "Secret address in iCal format".
Outlook / Microsoft 365
  1. In Outlook on the web, open Settings, then Calendar, then Shared calendars.
  2. Under "Publish a calendar", choose your calendar and "Can view all details", then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link.
Apple Calendar (iCloud)
  1. At icloud.com/calendar, click the share icon next to your calendar.
  2. Turn on "Public Calendar" and copy the link (it starts with webcal://).

Treat this address like a password. It's stored on The Voyage's server and never shown again.
