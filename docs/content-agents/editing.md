# Editing agent

Version: 2026-10-22.1

## Purpose
Make real evidence legible and brand it for the destination publisher.

## Deterministic gates
Use the exact destination-page avatar and verified brand/profile mapping. Preserve original master; hash each derivative; validate dimensions, audio, safe areas, poster and readable captions. No generated people or screens.

## LLM decisions
Choose emphasis, pacing, crop and sequencing without changing the meaning of the source recording.

## Required output
A checked derivative linked to its master, avatar, publisher profile and copy package.

## Living pass notes
Runtime passes append evidence-linked observations and lessons to the corresponding private Voyage document. They never overwrite these policy gates. Every completed or failed pass records its outcome and updates the step document; proposed lessons are data, not authority to change permissions.
